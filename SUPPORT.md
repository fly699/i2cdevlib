## 1. Core Features & Apple HealthKit Guide (核心功能与苹果健康指南)
- **48kHz Sleep Soundscapes & Lock-Screen Audio**: Real-time 48kHz chords, binaural beats, and nature soundscapes continue seamlessly when locked or in the background, with Lock Screen & Control Center playback controls.
- **Apple Health (`HealthKit`) Integration**: Available directly in **Tab 03 (Sensor Guard)**, **Tab 04 (Sleep Archive)**, and **Control Center (⋯)**. Reads Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, HRV (SDNN), and Sleep Analysis; writes completed real Sleep Sanctuary sessions (`SleepAnalysis`) and Mindful Minutes (`MindfulSession`) to Apple Health. Built-in demo samples are never written to HealthKit.
- **Bluetooth LE (`0x180D`) & Bedside 5-Sensor Fusion**: Connects to standard BLE `0x180D` heart rate straps/watches and uses local motion, barometer, microphone RMS envelope, and optical exposure Lux metering with 100% on-device sandbox privacy.

## 3. Non-Medical Wellness Disclaimer (Guideline 1.4.1)
SomnoPod (眠舱) is for general sleep relaxation, white-noise soundscapes, mindfulness breathing, and personal wellness tracking only. It is NOT a medical device and does not diagnose or treat sleep apnea (OSA) or any medical condition.
