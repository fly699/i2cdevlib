EarCast Pro Official Support & User Guide (官方支持中心)

1. How to Use Single-Ear Wireless Isolation (一耳远端收音 · 一耳佩戴监听)

Take both wireless stereo earbuds out of the charging case. Place the pickup earbud near the speaker/podium (its speaker is locked at digital zero 0x0000 so zero sound leaks and zero feedback howling occurs) and wear the other earbud in your ear to monitor up to 25x clarified speech. Tap Test Ear Tone while earbuds are connected to confirm which side is active.

2. 30-Second Pre-Roll & V2 SHA-256 Integrity Verification

Enable 30s Pre-Roll Buffer to keep a rolling 30-second RAM buffer while listening. When you tap Record, the prior 30 seconds are seamlessly prepended. Open the Vault tab and tap Proof Cert on any recording to inspect or copy its 3-layer V2 SHA-256 cryptographic integrity certificate.

3. Managing Subscriptions & Restoring Purchases

To restore a previous PRO unlock on a new device, open the PRO screen inside EarCast Pro and tap Restore Purchases. To manage or cancel an active subscription, open iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
