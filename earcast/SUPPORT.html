EarCast Pro Official Support & Feature Guide

1. True Wireless 1-Ear Remote Mic · 1-Ear Private Listen

Take both wireless earbuds out of the charging case. Place one earbud near the target sound source (speaker locked at 0x0000 silent to prevent feedback) and wear the other earbud in your ear to listen with up to 25x vocal clarity.

2. 30s Time-Machine Pre-Roll & V2 SHA-256 Forensic Verification

Enable 30s Pre-Roll to buffer the last 30 seconds in RAM. Every saved WAV/M4A/AAC file generates a companion .bookmarks.json V2 SHA-256 cryptographic seal that detects even a 1-bit alteration.
