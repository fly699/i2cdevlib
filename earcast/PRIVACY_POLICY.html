EarCast Pro Privacy Policy (隐私政策)

Effective & Last Updated: November 17, 2026  |  Developer: Resilient Shield (坚韧护盾)

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% On-Device Processing

EarCast Pro (com.earcast.pro) is architected as a strictly offline, local-first acoustic and audio recording application. We do NOT collect, transmit, upload, sell, rent, or store any personal data, voice recordings, microphone streams, Bluetooth identifiers, sensor telemetry, analytics, or advertising identifiers (IDFA) on any external server.

【耳界 EarCast Pro】采用严格的 100% 本机离线沙盒架构：我们不设任何云端服务器，不要求注册账号，绝不收集、上传、共享或出售您的任何录音音频、麦克风流、蓝牙外设信息、运动/气压传感器数据或个人身份信息。

2. Device Permissions & Strictly Local Purpose

  • Microphone (麦克风权限): Used solely in real time on your device for personal speech amplification, single-earbud remote pickup routing, 6-band parametric EQ, and user-initiated local audio recording (WAV/M4A/AAC). The optional 30-second Pre-Roll buffer is OFF by default and resides purely in volatile RAM until you explicitly tap Record.
  • Bluetooth (蓝牙权限): Used locally to enumerate and route audio between connected wireless earbuds/headsets (Single-Ear Isolation and Dual-Headset Split TX/RX).
  • Motion, Fitness & Barometer Sensors (运动与气压传感器权限): Processed exclusively in local RAM to detect physical movement of a remote pickup earbud while filtering out walking footsteps and door-closure air-pressure transients. Zero sensor data ever leaves your device.
  • Notifications (本地通知权限): Used locally to display active background audio monitoring or recording status so you always have transparent visibility.

3. Local Storage, User Control & Data Deletion

All recordings and their companion V2 SHA-256 integrity verification files (.bookmarks.json) are stored exclusively inside your device's local application sandbox (accessible via the in-app Recording Vault and the iOS Files app under On My iPhone / EarCast Pro). Deleting a recording in the app or uninstalling EarCast Pro permanently and irreversibly erases those files from your device.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Optional PRO unlocks (Annual Subscription with 3-Day Free Trial, Monthly Subscription, and Lifetime One-Time Purchase) are processed directly and securely by Apple StoreKit 2 / Google Play Billing. Resilient Shield never receives or stores your credit card or payment credentials.

5. Children's Privacy & Contact Us

Because EarCast Pro collects zero personal information from users of any age, no personal data from children under 13 (or applicable GDPR/CCPA age thresholds) is ever gathered. For any privacy questions, contact us at: t58@163.com.
