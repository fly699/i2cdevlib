SomnoPod (眠舱) Privacy Policy & HealthKit Disclosure

Applicable App / 适用应用: SomnoPod: Sleep Sounds & HRV / 眠舱 (com.somnopod.sleep.ios, com.somnopod.sleep.global, com.somnopod.sleep.china)
Data Controller & Developer / 开发者: CHUNHONG TANG
Privacy & Data Protection Contact / 隐私联系邮箱: t58@163.com

Core Privacy Commitment (核心隐私承诺): SomnoPod operates on a strict 100% On-Device Local Sandbox architecture. No account registration is required. We do NOT collect, upload, store on external servers, sell, or share your audio, physiological waveforms, HealthKit data, location, or personal identity with any third party, advertising network, or AI training pipeline.

1. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing (系统权限与本地处理说明)

Every permission below is optional and requested only when you enable the corresponding feature:

  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription / 麦克风权限): Used locally in real time to compute breathing airflow acoustic envelopes (RMS dB) and optionally record up to 8-second snore or sleep-talk highlight clips strictly inside your device's private sandbox. Zero audio is ever uploaded over the network; you can delete any or all clips in one tap inside Tab 04.
  • Motion & Barometer Sensors (NSMotionUsageDescription / 运动与气压传感器): Used locally to measure bedside mattress ballistocardiography (BCG micro-vibrations), chest breathing tilt, toss-and-turn actigraphy, and bedroom barometric pressure trends.
  • Front Optical Ambient Illuminance Metering (NSCameraUsageDescription / 环境光照度曝光参数测算): Active only while the Sleep Sanctuary screen is awake to read optical exposure duration and ISO metadata solely to estimate bedroom ambient light in Lux (helping advise melatonin-friendly darkness). It stops immediately when the screen dims or locks, and zero photos, pixel frames, or videos are ever captured, rendered, saved, or transmitted.
  • Bluetooth LE (NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription / 蓝牙心率外设权限): Used solely to connect standard Bluetooth LE 0x180D heart rate chest straps, armbands, or watches for real-time R-R interval HRV analysis and cardiac-entrained sleep soundscapes.
  • Apple HealthKit (NSHealthShareUsageDescription & NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription / 苹果健康读写权限):
    • Read (读取): Heart Rate (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRate), Resting Heart Rate (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierRestingHeartRate), Heart Rate Variability SDNN (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRateVariabilitySDNN), and Sleep Analysis (HKCategoryTypeIdentifierSleepAnalysis) to display your personal recovery baseline and drive adaptive sleep soundscapes.
    • Write (写入): Completed real Sleep Sanctuary sessions (HKCategoryTypeIdentifierSleepAnalysis) and Mindful Minutes (HKCategoryTypeIdentifierMindfulSession). Built-in demonstration records are strictly blocked from writing to HealthKit.
    • Apple Guideline 5.1.1 & HealthKit Compliance: HealthKit data is processed exclusively on your device, is NEVER uploaded to any external server, and is NEVER used for advertising, marketing, user profiling, data brokering, or third-party analytics.
  • Location While In Use (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription / 使用期间位置权限): Used locally while the app is in use solely to align local sunset/sunrise circadian cues and barometric altitude calibration. Coordinates are never uploaded or stored on any server.
  • Photos Add-Only & Media Library (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription & NSAppleMusicUsageDescription / 相册保存与媒体库混音): Photos write-only access is used only when you tap to save a generated sleep report poster to your Photo Library; Media Library access allows optional background music playback control alongside the 48kHz sleep synthesizer.

2. In-App Purchases, Zero Third-Party Tracking & Data Deletion (内购、零追踪与数据清除)

  • Apple StoreKit 2 Billing: All SomnoPod Pro subscriptions (com.somnopod.pro.monthly, com.somnopod.pro.yearly) and Lifetime Unlock (com.somnopod.pro.lifetime) are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2. SomnoPod never accesses or stores your credit card or payment details.
  • Zero Third-Party Ad / Tracking SDKs: SomnoPod contains zero advertising SDKs, zero cross-app tracking (IDFA), and zero third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.
  • Full User Control & Instant Deletion: You can delete individual sleep sessions, clear all snore highlight clips, or wipe all local sleep history at any time inside the app, or uninstall the app to permanently erase its entire local sandbox. You may also revoke HealthKit, Microphone, Bluetooth, Camera, or Location permissions anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
