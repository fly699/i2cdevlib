1. Quick Start & Core Features Guide (核心功能指南)

A. 48kHz Sleep Soundscapes & Lock-Screen Background Audio (后台锁屏助眠播放)

  • Tap the main Start Sleep button to launch real-time 48kHz chords, binaural beats, and natural white noise with customizable sleep fade-out timers.
  • Persistent Background Playback: Audio continues seamlessly when you lock your iPhone/iPad screen or return to the Home Screen, with full Play/Pause controls on the iOS Lock Screen and Control Center.

B. Apple Health (HealthKit) & Bluetooth LE 0x180D Heart Rate Sync (苹果健康与蓝牙心率同步)

  • Where to Find Apple HealthKit in App: Open Tab 03 (Sensor Guard / 传感守护), Tab 04 (Sleep Archive / 睡眠账本), or tap the top-right ⋯ Control Center button to access the dedicated Apple Health (HealthKit) Sleep & HRV Sync card.
  • Read & Write Types: With your permission, SomnoPod reads Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, Heart Rate Variability (SDNN), and Sleep Analysis from Apple Health, and writes completed real Sleep Sanctuary sessions (SleepAnalysis) and Mindful Minutes (MindfulSession) to Apple Health. Built-in demo records are never written to HealthKit.
  • Bluetooth LE 0x180D: Tap the Bluetooth HR radar button to pair standard BLE 0x180D chest straps or smartwatches broadcasting live heart rate.

C. Bedside 5-Sensor Sleep Tracking & Local Snore Sandbox (五源无感监测与本地鼾声沙盒)

  • Place your iPhone plugged into power 15–25 cm from your pillow on the mattress (or select HR-Only / Pure Audio L0 mode if placed on a nightstand).
  • Optional 8-second snore/sleep-talk highlight clips and all sleep waveforms are stored 100% locally inside your device's private sandbox with zero cloud upload, and can be deleted with one tap at any time.
